**Picture books, as designed.**

Fixed-layout books (picture books, many children's books, as EPUB or Kindle AZW3) are now read as real pages: each page exactly as the book designed it, with its pictures, fonts and words where they belong, scaled to your screen. Before, books like Geronimo Stilton's *Wedding Crashers* showed as stray lines of text on empty pages.

- Page turning with the page curl, two-page spreads, Scroll mode, zoom, bookmarks and offline downloads all work as they do for comics.
- Right-to-left books open right to left.
- Pages are cleaned on the server and shown where nothing in them can run, so a book can't reach out to the internet or run scripts.
- Fonts some EPUBs store scrambled (the standard font obfuscation, not copy protection) are unscrambled, so they show as intended.
- A picture book downloaded for offline reading before this update is downloaded again by itself, with its real pages.

Everything else is as in [0.1.5](https://github.com/Spinjitsudoom/Loft/releases/tag/v0.1.5).
