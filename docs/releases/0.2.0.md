**A library you borrow from: bound spines, checking out, ratings, going to a page, and an activity log.**

**Spines in their covers' colours.** In the stacks, every book stands like a bound hardback: cloth, raised ribs and a title label, in two colours taken from its own cover.
- The whole title fits: smaller, or on two or three lines.
- Books already in your library get their colours in the background after the update.

**Check books out.** Opening a book checks it out to you, and finishing it returns it.
- It's also returned when it's left unread for 30 days, or when you choose **Return** on its page. Returning early keeps your place.
- Nobody waits: everyone can still read a book someone has out. Its page says who has it.
- **Your library card** (in Your settings) lists what you have out and everything you've returned, with dates.

**Ratings.** Give a book one to five stars on its page, or when you reach the end.
- Everyone sees the library's average.
- Book lists can be sorted by **Highest rated**.

**Go to a page.** In the reader, tap "Page 12 of 340" (or press G) and type the page you want.

**Activity (for admins).** Library settings has an Activity page. It shows sign-ins, people joining, books added, edited and removed, loans and returns, ratings, requests, comments, and Loft's own work (scans, backups, updates).
- Newest first, with filters for a person and a kind. Entries are kept for a year.

**Fixed:**
- Long titles no longer push a book's page wider than a phone's screen.
- Kindle books show the title and author stored in the file.
- A brand-new library's first scan no longer fails.
- On Windows, the tray's Restart the server can no longer start a second server.

Everything else is as in [0.1.8](https://github.com/Spinjitsudoom/Loft/releases/tag/v0.1.8).
